Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering from SK GRIP PRO.

How do I place an order?
Open the Shop, add the products you want to your cart, then open your Cart and tap Order on WhatsApp. Your order list is prepared as a WhatsApp message, ready to send to us.
Can I pay on the website?
No. The website does not take payments. Orders are sent to us on WhatsApp, and we confirm the price, availability and shipping with you before the order is final.
Why do some products say "Price to be confirmed"?
The final price for that product has not been set yet. You can still add it to your cart or ask about it on WhatsApp, and we will confirm the price with you. Items without a price are not included in the cart total.
Is my cart saved?
Your cart is saved in your own browser on the device you are using. It stays there when you close the page, but it can be lost if you clear your browser data. It is not shared between devices.
How long does delivery take, and where do you deliver?
Delivery details are on our Shipping page. We also confirm shipping with you before your order is final. If you have a question, please contact us.
What if I want to return or change an order?
Please contact us as soon as possible and we will help you.
How can I contact SK GRIP PRO?
You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp or email. All our contact details are on the Contact page.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we will get back to you.
