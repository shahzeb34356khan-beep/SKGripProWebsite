Shipping Information

Everything you need to know about how your SK GRIP PRO order is processed, sent, tracked and handled if something goes wrong.

Important Shipping details, including available methods, costs and estimated delivery times, will be confirmed before you place your order. Please review them at checkout before completing your purchase.

Order Processing

Once you place an order, we review it and prepare it for dispatch. Here is what to expect from start to finish.

1

Order Received

After checkout, you will receive an order confirmation with a summary of your items.

2

Order Review & Preparation

We check your order details and prepare your items for dispatch.

3

Dispatch

When your parcel is handed to the carrier, we will let you know and share tracking information where available.

  • Please double-check your name, address and contact details before submitting your order.
  • If we need to confirm or correct any information, we will contact you using the details you provided.

Shipping Methods

The shipping methods available for your order depend on your delivery location and the items in your order. The options, costs and estimated timeframes that apply to you will be shown and confirmed before you place your order.

Confirmed Before You Buy

You will see the available shipping options for your address before payment is completed.

Delivery Address

Orders are sent to the address you provide at checkout. Accurate and complete details help avoid problems.

Carrier Handling

Your parcel is handed to a delivery carrier once it leaves us. Carrier service terms may apply.

Tracking Information

Where tracking is available for your chosen shipping method, we will share tracking details once your order has been dispatched.

  • Tracking details will be sent using the contact information provided with your order.
  • Tracking updates are supplied by the carrier, so it may take some time for new information to appear after dispatch.
  • Please keep your order confirmation and tracking details handy in case you need to contact us or the carrier.
  • If you have not received tracking details and expected them, please contact us and include your order information.

Delivery Delays

Sometimes a delivery takes longer than expected. Delays can be caused by factors outside our control, and delivery estimates are not guarantees.

Possible Causes

Carrier issues, high order volumes, weather or other disruptions, incorrect address details, or customs and local requirements for some destinations.

If Your Order Is Late

Check your tracking information first. If there is no progress or something looks wrong, contact us with your order details and we will look into it.

Missed Deliveries

If a carrier cannot deliver your parcel, follow any notice they leave and contact us if you need assistance.

Damaged or Problem Parcels

We want your SK GRIP PRO order to reach you in good condition. If something arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, please contact us as soon as possible so we can help.

  • Take clear photos of the outer packaging, the shipping label and the affected item(s).
  • Keep all packaging materials until the issue has been reviewed.
  • Contact us with your order details and the photos so we can assess the situation.
  • If the damage is visible on delivery, consider noting it with the carrier where possible.
  • We will review each case and let you know the next steps in line with our store policies.
