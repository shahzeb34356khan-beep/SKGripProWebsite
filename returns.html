SK GRIP PRO

Returns & Refunds

How to request a return or refund, what to do if your order arrives damaged or incorrect, and what to expect once your request is reviewed.

Important Final return and refund conditions will be confirmed before purchases begin. Please review the conditions that apply to your order before completing your purchase.

How to Request a Return or Refund

If you would like to request a return or refund, follow the steps below. Each request is reviewed individually.

1

Contact Us

Get in touch through our contact page and tell us you would like to request a return or refund.

2

Share Your Order Details

Include your order information, the item(s) concerned and the reason for your request. Photos may help where relevant.

3

Review & Instructions

We will review your request and let you know the next steps, including whether the item needs to be sent back.

  • Please keep your order confirmation and any related messages until your request is resolved.
  • Please do not send an item back until we have confirmed the next steps with you.

Damaged Products

If your SK GRIP PRO product arrives damaged, please contact us as soon as possible so we can look into it.

  • Take clear photos of the damaged item, the packaging and the shipping label.
  • Keep all packaging materials until your request has been reviewed.
  • Send us your order details together with the photos through our contact page.
  • We will review the case and let you know the available options in line with the conditions confirmed for your purchase.

Incorrect Orders

If you receive an item that does not match what you ordered, or your order is incomplete, please let us know so we can help.

Check Your Order

Compare the items received with your order confirmation to identify what is different or missing.

Tell Us What Happened

Contact us with your order details, a description of the issue and photos of the items and packaging.

We Review & Respond

We will review your request and explain the next steps for resolving the issue.

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, we will confirm the details with you. Refund eligibility is assessed case by case and depends on the conditions confirmed for your purchase.

  • Approved refunds are generally returned using the original payment method, unless we agree another option with you.
  • We will notify you once your request has been reviewed and a decision has been made.
  • The time it takes for funds to appear in your account may depend on your payment provider or bank.
  • Where a return is required, your request may be completed after the returned item has been received and checked.

Return Shipping

If we ask you to send an item back, we will provide instructions first. Details about who covers return shipping costs will be confirmed as part of the conditions that apply to your order.

Wait for Instructions

Please contact us before returning anything so we can confirm the correct process and return address.

Pack It Securely

Use suitable packaging to protect the item during transit and include any details we ask for.

Keep Proof of Postage

Keep your receipt and any tracking details until the return has been received and your request is resolved.

Need Help With a Return?

Have a question about returns or refunds, or ready to submit a request? Our team is here to help.
